# Team [Insert Team Name] Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment for our DevOps group project, we as team members pledge to make participation in our team a harassment-free, respectful, and collaborative experience for everyone. Our primary goal is to successfully deliver the Population Reporting System while adhering to Scrum and DevOps best practices.

## 2. Standards of Behavior
Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:
* **Respect:** Using welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, and gracefully accepting constructive criticism during code reviews.
* **Collaboration:** Actively participating in Scrum ceremonies (Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Reviews) and sharing knowledge freely.
* **Reliability:** Meeting agreed-upon Sprint goals, deadlines, and attending scheduled meetings on time.
* **DevOps Discipline:** Strictly adhering to our GitFlow branching strategy, writing clean and commented code, and ensuring all commits are linked to GitHub Issues.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* "Freeriding" or failing to contribute meaningfully to the agreed Sprint tasks.
* Merging code directly to `master` or `develop` without a Pull Request and peer review.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## 3. Agile & Meeting Attendance
* **Code Reviews & Live Sessions:** Attendance at the two graded Code Reviews (Weeks 6/7 and 12/13) is mandatory. Being late or unprepared caps the group grade at 40%; absence results in a 0%.
* **Communication:** If a team member cannot attend a meeting due to time zones, work commitments, or personal reasons, they must notify the team via [Slack/Teams/Discord] at least 24 hours in advance.
* **Task Management:** All tasks must be tracked via our Kanban board (GitHub Projects). Moving a card to "Done" requires the code to be merged and tested.

## 4. Mitigating Circumstances & Dispute Resolution
We understand that life happens. If a team member is facing personal, medical, or academic difficulties:
1. They should inform the team as soon as possible so workloads can be adjusted temporarily.
2. If the issue impacts their ability to contribute long-term, they must follow the university's official mitigating circumstances procedures.

## 5. Disciplinary Procedures & Dismissal
As per the SET09803 Module Brief, severe or repeated breaches of this Code of Conduct may result in dismissal from the team. The process is as follows:
1. **Evidence:** The team will document evidence of the breach (e.g., GitHub metrics, missed meetings, ignored messages).
2. **Presentation:** Evidence is presented to a member of the module delivery team (Kevin Sim or the assigned Tutor).
3. **Mitigation:** The accused individual will have the opportunity to present mitigating circumstances to the group or privately to the module delivery team.
4. **Decision:** The module delivery team retains the right to the final decision on whether dismissal is warranted.
5. **Appeal:** Any dismissed team member has one week to appeal the decision to the module team with suitable evidence.
   *Note: Dismissal from the team means the individual cannot deliver the coursework and will fail the module.*

## 6. Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the module leader at [Insert Tutor Email]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
